# Community outcome taxonomy

## Audience
Followers, readers, viewers, subscribers, and lightweight reactions.

## Participation
People who ask, answer, contribute, review, or attend.

## Reciprocity
Member-to-member replies, help, introductions, and shared responsibility.

## Continuity
Returning participants, durable threads, maintained artifacts, and rituals that remain useful.

## Mission outcomes
Research evidence, product requirements, collaborations, issues, reviews, pull requests, and shared infrastructure.

## Attribution
Direct when the source or path is known, inferred when evidence is suggestive, unknown otherwise. Never present audience size or reaction volume as proof of community.
