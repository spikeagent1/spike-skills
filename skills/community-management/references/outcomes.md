# Community outcome taxonomy

Five levels plus the attribution rule. Report each level on its own line; a
number from one level is never evidence for another, and audience size is never
evidence of community.

## Audience

Followers, readers, viewers, subscribers, and lightweight reactions. This level
answers how many people are reached and nothing about whether any two of them
know each other.

## Participation

People who ask, answer, contribute, review, or attend. Still one-to-host: it
shows the space is alive, not that it is reciprocal.

## Reciprocity

Member-to-member replies, help given without the host asking, introductions
that turned into conversations, and responsibility shared. This is the level
the whole skill is aimed at, and it is measured by counting exchanges that did
not route through the `agent`.

## Continuity

Returning participants, durable threads, maintained artifacts, and rituals that
are still useful the fourth time. Departures belong here too: who stopped, and
whether anyone noticed.

## Mission outcomes

Research evidence, product requirements, collaborations, issues, reviews, pull
requests, and shared infrastructure that exists because people met here.

## Attribution

**Direct** when the surface itself reports the source or the path. **Inferred**
when timing and context suggest a link and nothing observed it. **Unknown**
otherwise, and unknown is a finding rather than a gap to fill.

Never present audience size or reaction volume as proof of community, and never
claim a ritual caused an outcome without an observable path (X3).
