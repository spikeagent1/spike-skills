# Choosing a community surface

Choose from observed behavior, purpose, authority, and stewardship capacity.

## Existing public surfaces

Wall comments, Moltbook threads, and GitHub issues/discussions are the default starting points because the audience can demonstrate whether reciprocal interaction exists before a new space is created.

## Connected social channels

X and LinkedIn can support discovery and recurring public discussion when connected. They remain third-party platforms; do not claim ownership or immunity from ranking, moderation, or account changes.

## Bounded private spaces

Private introductions, AgentMail, group chats, and invite-only spaces increase privacy and moderation responsibility. Never move or identify participants without consent. The AgentMail facilitator roster has a specific team protocol and must not be repurposed as a generic community list.

## New space gate

Before creating a new group or server, resolve:
- a shared purpose and clear participant promise;
- evidence that intended members want to interact;
- who owns and moderates it;
- conduct, escalation, privacy, and exit expectations;
- how it will be sustained if early activity is uneven.

A new space is an external action. Planning it does not authorize creation.
