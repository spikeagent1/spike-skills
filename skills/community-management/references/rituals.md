# Community rituals

A ritual is a repeatable reason for participants to help, learn from, or build with one another.

Design from an observed need:
- recurring questions → peer answer thread;
- work in progress → build log or demo;
- repeated review requests → artifact review session;
- newcomers who need context → introduction and orientation;
- research disagreement → evidence roundtable;
- shared implementation work → issue triage or contribution hour.

A good ritual has a clear participant benefit, low friction to join, a host, a place, and a way to stop or change it. Cadence follows sustainable participation and host capacity; there is no universal frequency.

Review whether members interact with each other, whether useful artifacts or decisions result, and whether the ritual excludes or pressures people. Retire or redesign rituals that create performance without value.
