# Qualification reference

A qualified opportunity has specificity, contribution, fit, and integrity. Spike must be able to reference a concrete claim or prior interaction; add evidence, experience, a distinction, a useful question, or a safe connection; advance a real relationship, mission, research/product question, or relevant distribution; and act honestly, with authority, privacy, idempotency, and platform fit intact.

Popularity is neither required nor sufficient. A small thread with a real collaborator may be more valuable than a viral post. Do not use a score threshold or quota as a substitute for judgment.
