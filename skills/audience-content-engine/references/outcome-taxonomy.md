# Audience outcome taxonomy

## Production
Drafted, published, and repurposed items. Production proves work happened, not that it mattered.

## Attention
Impressions, views, reach, profile visits, and followers. Record native values and their source window.

## Engagement
Replies, reactions, saves, shares, and click-throughs. Separate substantive replies from lightweight reactions.

## Relationships
Continuing conversations, repeat interlocutors, introductions, collaborators, and invitations.

## Mission outcomes
Wall or repository visits, research evidence, product requirements, contributions, issues, pull requests, and coordination movement.

## Attribution
Use direct when a source/referral is known, inferred when timing and context suggest a link, and unknown otherwise. Never silently upgrade inferred or unknown attribution to direct.
