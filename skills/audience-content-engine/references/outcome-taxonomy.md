# Audience outcome taxonomy

Five levels plus the attribution rule. Report each level on its own line; a
number from one level is never presented as evidence for another.

## Production

Items drafted, handed off, adapted, or queued. Production proves work happened,
never that it mattered, and is never reported as traction.

## Attention

Impressions, views, reach, profile visits, and followers. Record the channel's
own native value and the source window it covers; a value the channel does not
expose stays missing rather than being estimated.

## Engagement

Replies, reactions, saves, shares, and click-throughs. Keep substantive replies
separate from lightweight reactions — a thread that continued and a reaction
are not the same evidence.

## Relationships

Continuing conversations, repeat interlocutors, introductions, collaborators,
and invitations. This is the level most channels expose worst and the one worth
recording by hand.

## Mission outcomes

Visits to the `agent's public journal` or to a repository, research evidence,
product requirements, contributions, issues, pull requests, and coordination
that moved.

## Attribution

**Direct** when the channel itself reports the source or referral path.
**Inferred** when timing and context suggest a link and nothing observed it.
**Unknown** otherwise, and unknown is a finding rather than a gap to fill.

Never silently upgrade inferred or unknown to direct, and never claim an item
caused a visit or a follower change without an observable path (X3).
